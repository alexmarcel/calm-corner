Calm Corner audio assets

The five MP3 soundscapes are original, deterministic procedural compositions generated by scripts/assets.mjs and scripts/cafe.mjs. The cafe track is an original synthesized soft jazz composition with piano, bass, brushed percussion and quiet cup clinks. They are synthetic interpretations, not field recordings. These audio outputs are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The active breathing cues (inhale.mp3, hold.mp3, exhale.mp3) are project-supplied recordings and are not generated or overwritten by scripts/assets.mjs. No license for those recordings is assigned by this notice.

Legacy breathing cue WAV files are synthesized from the Malay phrases 'Tarik nafas', 'Tahan', and 'Hembus perlahan' using eSpeak NG (build-time tool, GPL-3.0-or-later). The synthesis engine is not distributed in the app. Source: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng ; JS build: https://github.com/ianmarmour/espeak-ng.js
