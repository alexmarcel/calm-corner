Calm Corner audio assets

The five MP3 soundscapes are original, deterministic procedural compositions generated by scripts/assets.mjs. They are synthetic interpretations, not field recordings. These audio outputs are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Breathing cue WAV files are synthesized from the Malay phrases 'Tarik nafas', 'Tahan', and 'Hembus perlahan' using eSpeak NG (build-time tool, GPL-3.0-or-later). The synthesis engine is not distributed in the app. Source: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng ; JS build: https://github.com/ianmarmour/espeak-ng.js
